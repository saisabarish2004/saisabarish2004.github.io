About

I work close to the hardware, but I own the whole product path — scoping what a device actually needs to do, architecting the firmware for it, and validating it against real timing and signal constraints, not just simulations. My work spans STM32, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and MATLAB/Simulink, applied to EV, ADAS, safety, and IoT products that have to hold up outside a lab.

Since 2025 I've run my own practice as an embedded product developer and technical trainer: taking client firmware from a rough brief through to a deployed, working system, and training engineers in embedded systems and FreeRTOS along the way. I'm reliable on scope and timelines because I've had to be — my name is the only thing backing the delivery date.
